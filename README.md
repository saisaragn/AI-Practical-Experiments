# Artificial Intelligence | Practical Experiments

A hands-on learning repository exploring fundamental Artificial Intelligence concepts, algorithms, problem-solving techniques, and intelligent systems through 12 laboratory experiments.

Developed as part of the **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML)** curriculum at **Marwadi University**.

## Areas of Exploration

- **Intelligent Agents:** Reflex agents and goal-based agents
- **Search Algorithms:** BFS, DFS, UCS and A* Search
- **Game AI:** Minimax and Alpha-Beta Pruning
- **Knowledge Representation:** Rule-based systems and logical inference
- **Constraint Satisfaction:** Backtracking and consistency checking
- **Probabilistic AI:** Bayesian Networks and probabilistic reasoning
- **AI Planning:** STRIPS and Goal-Stack Planning
- **Decision Making:** Value Iteration and sequential decision processes
- **Machine Learning:** k-NN, Decision Trees, Naive Bayes and Perceptron
- **Responsible AI:** Ethics and explainability
